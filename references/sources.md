# Public style references

Reviewed November 16, 2026. Select up to three style references for a draft by audience and purpose; factual verification may require additional sources. The observations below are editorial inferences from the inspected material, not an official FedRAMP style guide. These pointers contain no cached source prose or transcripts.

## Rules and precise explanations

### Using the FedRAMP Consolidated Rules
https://www.fedramp.gov/2026/rules/

Live guide inspected November 16, 2026; replaces the original preview-host link. Use for actor-led requirements, defined terms, requirement force, and stable identifiers paired with readable names. Read the definitions and surrounding applicability before interpreting a rule. Its illustrative examples are not independent requirements.

## Announcements and operational notices

### Memo to Milestone: FedRAMP Grants the First Full FedRAMP 20x Class C Certification
https://www.fedramp.gov/2026-11-02-memo-to-milestone/

Published November 2, 2026; full article inspected. Institutional prose, not attributed to Waterman personally. Useful for connecting an originating objective to work delivered, demonstrating an abstract claim with an operational example, and acknowledging missing data and work still ahead. Keep limited pilot results distinct from full certification. Embedded OMB quotations have their own authorship and authority.

### NTC-0017: VDR/VER Migration Progress Reporting and Upcoming FY27 Q1 FedRAMP Security Inbox Test
https://www.fedramp.gov/notices/0017/

Published November 10, 2026; full notice inspected. Use for audience-first operational communication, practical support alongside consequences, explicit exemptions, and conditional versus likely dates. Its terminology note supports avoiding unnecessary acronyms while allowing defined shorthand for repeated long names. Reverify dates and conditions before reuse.

### NTC-0018: Updates on the FedRAMP Board and Activities Proposed in RFC-0032, RFC-0033 & RFC-0034
https://www.fedramp.gov/notices/0018/

Published November 16, 2026; full notice inspected. Use for an informational opening that says no action is required, explaining disagreement without obscuring the decision, acknowledging authority limits, and identifying revised plans and the next expected update. Institutional prose; do not ascribe it personally to Waterman.

### NTC-0019: Outcome of RFC-0032 Offerings By Government & Phase 4 Class G Pilot Announcement
https://www.fedramp.gov/notices/0019/

Published November 16, 2026; full notice inspected. Use for audience boundaries and a concise outcome summary. Particularly useful for preserving statement-level status: the notice announces a decision and a future pilot but explicitly labels its included rule text as a preview. Publication alone does not make every statement a final requirement.

### Propelling Change: FedRAMP Launches Consolidated Rules for 2026
https://www.fedramp.gov/2026-06-25-propelling-change-fedramp-launches-consolidated-rules-for-2026/

Published June 25, 2026; inspected during the October 28 review. Retained for approachable launch announcements and calls to participate. Its dates and transition descriptions are historical, not a current baseline.

## Pete Waterman: attributable writing and speech

### FedRAMP Director's Letter to the Board, within “Announcing the initial 20x Phase 2 pilot participants and FedRAMP’s next steps”
https://www.fedramp.gov/2025-12-10-announcing-the-initial-20x-phase-2-pilot-participants/

Letter sent December 9, 2025; published December 10; full embedded letter inspected November 16, 2026. Select the signed letter, not the surrounding announcement. Strongest direct writing reference here for candid leadership communication: delivered results, unmet needs, concrete actions, collaborators, and phased delivery. Do not transplant its personal anecdotes, signature, historical terminology, or commitments.

### FedRAMP 20x Is Recasting Compliance as Continuous Trust
https://fedgovtoday.com/innovation-in-govt/fedramp-20x-is-recasting-compliance-as-continuous-trust

Published July 30, 2026 (date verified in the publisher's AMP version); article inspected November 16. Contains directly attributed Waterman and Ryan Hoesing quotations within reporter prose. Use only Waterman's attributed words for his phrasing; the MFA screenshot example is Hoesing's. Useful for concrete operational triggers, observable results, and explaining each participant's responsibility. The article was read; the embedded recording was not watched. This is an older source newly added to the skill, not a newly published interview.
